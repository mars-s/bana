# Asset attribution

Every non-code asset in this repository is listed here with its author, source and licence.

**Policy:** TanaCraft contains no Tana icons, images, fonts, CSS or other assets. Every asset is
original work by TanaCraft contributors or third-party material under an open licence. Screenshots of
other vendors' software are never committed.

Generated-in-code art is original and has no file to list: bullets, chevrons, tag chips and the
sidebar glyphs are painted by `crates/ui-egui`.
